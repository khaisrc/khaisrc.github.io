## Problem

Pharmacists performing pre-purchase verification had to scroll, click, and mentally align information spread across the page. Field ordering and visual highlights added cognitive load to a compliance decision that needed to remain both careful and efficient.

## Contribution

I delivered a series of backend and frontend improvements, culminating in a redesigned review layout developed with direct feedback from pharmacy users.

## Technical approach

- Placed source and entered prescription data side by side for faster comparison.
- Added keyboard actions for common decisions and a resizable review panel.
- Carried forward applicable prior review information to avoid redundant work.
- Added activity history and clearer context for automated and manual outcomes.
- Removed controls and visual treatments that did not contribute to the decision.

## Outcome

The redesign reduced unnecessary interaction and made the comparison task more direct. The available source material contains baseline timing and projected savings, but no verified post-launch result, so those projections are intentionally not presented as impact.

## Engineering judgment

In a regulated workflow, speed cannot come from hiding decision context. The design concentrated relevant evidence, shortened navigation, and preserved an explicit review path.
